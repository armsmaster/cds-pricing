User guide

Three tools, switched with the tabs at the top:

  • Rate curve — bootstrap a RUONIA zero-coupon curve from OIS bid/ask quotes.
  • Credit curves — bootstrap an issuer's hazard-rate curve from its bond prices.
  • CDS Pricing — price standardised CDS contracts (100 bp fixed coupon, quarterly IMM schedule) against stored rate and credit curves.

Rate curve

  1. Pick a trade date and paste OIS quotes (JSON array of { "rate", "tenor", "bid", "ask" }, bid/ask in %). The par chart previews live.
  2. Set the max adjustment cap and press Calculate (Ctrl/Cmd+Enter).
  3. Press Save curve for credit use to store it — both credit curves and CDS pricing discount off a saved rate curve.

The fit is reliability-weighted (by bid/ask spread) with a forward-slope smoothness penalty capped at the average adjustment.

Credit curves

  1. Create an issuer and set its recovery rate R (LGD = 1 − R).
  2. Add the issuer's bonds by ISIN — reference data and coupon schedules are fetched from MOEX ISS and stored.
  3. In the Market prices table, review the fetched prices; edit a clean price to override it, or untick Include to drop a bond. Edits are saved per trade date.
  4. Pick a trade date and a saved risk-free curve, then Calculate credit curve.

Bonds are priced to their offer/put date. Market dirty prices come from a robust blend of bid/ask, last and WAP; each bond is weighted by how reliable its price is. The hazard curve is bootstrapped so model dirty prices match the market. Download Excel on the repricing table exports a macro-free workbook that rebuilds each model price with live formulas.

CDS Pricing

  1. Pick a trade date and a saved rate curve. The system fetches the stored rate and credit curves for all issuers with bond data.
  2. Filter by issuer (Ctrl/Cmd-click for multiple) or toggle Standard tenors only (6m, 1y, 2y, 3y, 5y, 7y, 10y).
  3. Press Price CDS. The results table shows all quarterly contracts from 3m up to the credit curve's horizon. Results are cached per trade date and auto-invalidated when the underlying curves change.
  4. Click any row to expand an inline detail panel with the full pricing breakdown: pricing summary (legs, RPV01, recovery), interest-rate and hazard-rate charts, per-period cashflows (DF, Q, premium PV, protection PV), and DV01/CDV01.
  5. Download the results table or a per-contract detail breakdown as Excel via the Download Excel / Download detail buttons.

The ISDA-standard model prices each CDS with a 100 bp fixed coupon on quarterly IMM dates (20 Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec). The upfront compensates for the difference between the fixed coupon and the market par spread; the rebate covers the pre-trade accrued fraction. Premium leg accounts for accrued-at-default (half the quarterly coupon on average). Sensitivity is measured by DV01 (1 bp rate bump) and Credit DV01 (1 bp hazard bump).

Notes

  • Shipped indices: RUONIA (RUB), SOFR (USD). Bonds are RUB.
  • Holidays are seeded for 2024–2026; outside that range a weekend-only calendar is used.
  • Bonds with no usable price on the trade date are skipped and listed under the table.
  • CDS results are cached per (rate curve, issuer, trade date). They auto-refresh when the rate curve or the issuer's bond data changes. Press Price CDS again to force a refresh.
